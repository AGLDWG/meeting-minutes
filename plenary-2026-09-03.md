### 4. Presentation

#### **Flores Bakker** from the Dutch Ministry of Finance on "Knowledge driven government"

Flores Bakker discusses how government services can be improved by proactively managing a "body of knowledge." 
This body consists of essential, interrelated domains: norms, organisation, process, user, information, application, 
and technology. For optimal service delivery, these domains need to align and complement each other, enabling 
balanced, integrated, and high-quality digital operations.
